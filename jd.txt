PROJECT FOR A TECHNICAL SERVICE AND WARRANTY SYSTEM

The objective of this project is to develop a system that allows for the proper administration of our technical service and warranty services. It will also include a public database detailing the problems and solutions submitted for technical service. This will enable us to feature these problems and solutions in a technical service blog, generating more traffic to our website and attracting new clients.

It is important that the system be flexible, allowing for the addition of areas and new options, similar to the points system implemented.

The system must allow clients to check the status and progress of their order.

The system must communicate with clients via email and WhatsApp API, providing updates on the order's progress at each stage of the process.

The system must alert technicians for orders that are longer than initially estimated, ensuring constant communication with the client.

The system must calculate revenue by sales category for technical services. This information should only be visible to Admin and Super Admin users.

The system must allow for the generation of reports that enable the proper management of technical services.

The system must connect to the Perseo system API to perform various actions, such as: calling clients using only their ID number; adding products to be used in the system, along with their code, price, and description; contacting suppliers to process warranty claims; and recording the serial numbers of entered products.

The system must send automatic messages after 30 days of the equipment being in the delivery area, and after 60 days, indicating that the product needs to be picked up and that failure to do so may result in it being considered abandoned.

When the equipment is transferred to a third-party technician, the system must allow the technician to be charged the repair cost.

The system must allow for the entry of computer equipment, enabling the tracking of the order status.

The system must allow the assignment of areas, such as:

Entrance Area
Maintenance Area
Outsourced Area
Warehouse Area
Delivery Area
Spare Parts Waiting Area

USER ROLES
The system must have the following roles:

Super Administrator
Administrator
Cashiers
Lead Technician
Outsourced Technician
Customers
